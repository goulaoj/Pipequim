# Pipequim — Computational Quantum Mechanics

A computational quantum mechanics project developed in Python as part of the *Quantum Mechanics II* course at Instituto Superior Técnico, University of Lisbon.

The project investigates the quantum behaviour of **two identical particles**, exploring how particle statistics and spin affect their wavefunctions, probability distributions, and physical observables.

## Overview

In quantum mechanics, identical particles are described by wavefunctions with specific symmetry properties under particle exchange. These properties distinguish bosons from fermions and play a fundamental role in determining their physical behaviour.

This project uses numerical methods and computational visualisation to study these effects in two-particle quantum systems.

## Main topics

- **Identical particles:** exchange symmetry and the construction of two-particle states.
- **Bosons and fermions:** symmetric and antisymmetric spatial wavefunctions.
- **Spin states:** the relationship between spin configurations and particle statistics.
- **Probability distributions:** visualisation and analysis of quantum probability densities.
- **Quantum correlations:** investigation of correlations between the positions of two particles.
- **Physical observables:** numerical evaluation of expectation values and other relevant quantities.

## Technologies

- **Python**
- **NumPy** — numerical calculations and array operations
- **Matplotlib** — plotting and visualisation of quantum states and probability distributions

## Repository contents

The repository contains the computational implementation and the project report, documenting the physical framework, numerical analysis, and results.
